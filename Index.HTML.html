Pre-Run Checklist
1.Inspect tooling for wear, marks, or damage Lightly brush the chuck lips and all roller profiles. Confirm there is no foreign material present.
2.Check seaming roll bearings Rotate seaming rolls checking for tight/loose bearings and any grinding. Bearings should rotate approximately ½ turn when spun with 1 finger. Under greased = spins too much. Over greased = does not spin enough.
3.Check chuck bearings (seamers disabled) Disable seamers and rotate chucks, checking for tight/loose bearings and any grinding.
4.Confirm chuck rotation on main die axis Actuate the main die axis and confirm that the chuck rotates properly.
5.Check gearboxes — rolls toward chuck (seamers disabled) Disable seamers and manually move seaming rolls towards the chuck. Confirm that the gearboxes are not tight or grinding.
6.Actuate lifters — confirm smooth single stroke Actuate lifters and confirm they are moving in a single, smooth stroke with no hesitation or stuttering.
7.Check pucks — spin freely, no looseness Lift the pucks and turn by hand. Confirm that they spin freely and are not loose or wobbly.
8.Chuck resistance and spring-back (seamers enabled) Enable seamers and turn chucks by hand. Chuck should have resistance and spring back into original position.
9.Run 2nd op seam test — no can present Run a 2nd operation seam test without a can. Confirm that the rollers are not making contact with the chuck.
